Frequently Asked Questions

Understanding the speech therapy journey doesn't have to be stressful. We've compiled the most common questions to help you feel confident before your first session.

Here to listen.

Our specialists are ready to help you find your voice.

health_and_safety

Do you accept insurance?

expand_more

We work with major insurance providers including BlueCross, Aetna, and Cigna. For out-of-network plans, we can provide detailed superbills that you may submit for reimbursement. Please contact our billing department for a complimentary benefits check.

schedule

How long is a typical session?

expand_more

Initial evaluations typically last 60 minutes. Ongoing therapy sessions are usually 30 to 45 minutes, depending on the client's age, attention span, and specific therapy goals. We value quality over quantity to ensure progress without fatigue.

diversity_3

What ages do you treat?

expand_more

We treat individuals across the lifespan. Our pediatric specialists work with toddlers as young as 18 months for early intervention, while our adult specialists focus on rehabilitation after trauma, stroke, or for accent modification and professional voice coaching.

menu_book

Is there homework between sessions?

expand_more

Yes, consistent practice is key to rewiring neural pathways for speech. We provide manageable, engaging home exercises—often just 5-10 minutes a day—tailored to fit your lifestyle so that progress continues outside of the clinic.

mic

How do you help with stuttering or mutism?

expand_more

We use a holistic, evidence-based approach. For stuttering, we focus on fluency shaping and stuttering modification techniques alongside confidence building. For selective mutism, we create a low-pressure environment to gradually increase communication comfort using behavioral therapy strategies.

Still have questions?

We understand that every voice is unique. Reach out to our team directly for a personalized consultation or to discuss specific concerns.
