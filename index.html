Frequently Asked Questions

Understanding the speech therapy journey doesn't have to be stressful. We've compiled answers to the most common questions so you can feel confident before your first session.

Do you accept insurance?

We accept a selection of insurance plans; please contact our intake team and we will verify your coverage for specific programs.

How long are sessions?

Typical sessions run 30–60 minutes depending on the treatment plan; our clinicians will recommend an optimal cadence during your intake.

What ages do you treat?

We provide services for children and adults — our programs are pediatric play-based therapy to adult functional communication recovery.

Still have questions?

Reach out to our team directly for a personalized consultation or to discuss specific concerns.
